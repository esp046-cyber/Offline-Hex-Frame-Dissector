Privacy

DNP3 FieldAssist runs entirely in your browser. Hex strings you paste are decoded locally and are never uploaded.

The last 50 decoded strings are saved in your browser's IndexedDB so you can reopen them offline. Use Delete saved history on the main page to remove them, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

The app sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts and uses no analytics.
